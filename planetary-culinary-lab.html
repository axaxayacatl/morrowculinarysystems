03 / Model documentation

What is being quantified?

01 / Sensory complexity

A constructed index combines saturating effects of ingredient count and preparation methods with a synthetic interaction penalty. It is a mathematical proxy, not a validated psychometric measure.

02 / Environmental feasibility

A weighted constraint score evaluates temperature, pressure, gravity, water, energy, and radiation against broad illustrative cultivation assumptions. It does not simulate complete planetary biology or atmospheric chemistry.

03 / Diversity and objective

Diversity is estimated from assumed category coverage. The objective combines complexity and feasibility according to the selected weight. These are exploratory modeling choices to be tested, not scientific laws.

RESEARCH LIMITATIONSREAD BEFORE INTERPRETING

All observations generated here are synthetic and determined by explicit formulas and a reproducible random seed. No actual tasting panel, measured aroma dataset, validated crop model, or astronomical observation is used.

For empirical research, replace the synthetic sensory generator with documented ingredient chemistry or structured tasting observations. Replace the illustrative feasibility score with crop-specific tolerance data, pressure and atmospheric models, radiation shielding assumptions, and resource budgets. Preserve source citations, units, uncertainty, and missing values.

The purpose of this companion is to explore a computational research design and expose assumptions—not to present fabricated results as scientific findings.
